# Product Goal

## Problem

Palworld has many materials, items, Pals, work roles, technologies, and progression paths. Players often do not know:

- what a newly acquired item is for
- whether an item is useful now or only later
- where to obtain a missing material
- which Pal fits a base or party role
- what goal is most valuable next

Switching to external wikis and tutorials breaks play flow and often provides generic advice that does not match the player's current state.

## Product

Palworld Guider is an accessible, state-aware advisor. It answers questions in short, concrete terms and personalizes them when player state is available.

The product is not an autopilot. It explains options and recommends next steps; the player remains responsible for executing them.

## Core User Questions

### Inventory Questions

- What is this item for?
- Should I keep, use, sell, or store it?
- Is it useful for my current goal?
- What can I craft right now?

### Material Questions

- How do I obtain this material?
- What is the fastest or most reliable source?
- What else does this material unlock?
- What am I still missing?

### Pal Questions

- Which Pal suits this work role?
- What does this Pal contribute to my base?
- Does my party have a work gap?
- Which Pal should I prioritize next?

### Progression Questions

- What should I do next?
- What goal is realistic right now?
- What technology or infrastructure should I unlock next?
- What are the tradeoffs between possible routes?

## Answer Standard

A good guide answer:

- directly addresses the question
- uses reviewed game data
- distinguishes fact from inference
- identifies missing or conflicting data
- references relevant player state when available
- offers a small number of practical next steps
- stays short enough for chat

## Success Criteria

The project succeeds when a player can ask common progression or inventory questions in game and receive a trustworthy, personalized, concise answer without opening a browser.
